1. Token pair

currency0 is the lower address. Symbols are read from each token's symbol().

Every 30 s the page reads the PoolManager's Swap logs of the window for fee / tick spacing (0,1) (0,10) (500,10) (2500,25) (3000,60) (10000,200) (30000,600). Price = 1.0001^tick of the latest swap, shown as currency1 per currency0 after decimals. No swap in the window means inactive. A new window applies from the next poll, with no restart. On an RPC error the page waits 60 s and keeps polling.

V3 discovers fee tiers 0.01%, 0.05%, 0.30% and 1.00% once per pair change, scans their Swap logs together, and checks active liquidity. Both uses the same block window for both protocols.

Manual mode reads each pool's token0(), token1(), fee(), slot0() and liquidity() with plain eth_call once per click of READ PRICES (10 calls, one at a time, never a batch, never eth_getLogs, no polling), plus symbol() and decimals() of the two tokens once. Price = 1.0001^tick of slot0, shown as token1 per token0 after decimals. Both pools must share token0 and token1 and have sqrtPriceX96 above 0. A pool with liquidity() = 0 is shown EMPTY and cannot be executed.

All pairs watches Token A against every Token B in the list. Once per list change the page discovers the V3 pools (factory.getPool for 0.01%, 0.05%, 0.30%, 1.00%) and the V4 pools with liquidity (getLiquidity through extsload for the seven fee tiers) of every pair. Every 30 s it sends ONE eth_getLogs over all V3 pools and ONE over the PoolManager, filtered to our pool ids, in a single batch, then the usual health batch. Only same-pair spreads are listed (cross-pair combinations are not displayed); they execute exactly as in Auto-scan.

Cross-DEX Prices

DEXFeePriceLiquiditySpread vs UniStatus

Not started.

Read-only (job 9). Every 30 s — once per READ PRICES in Manual mode — the page asks each configured DEX factory for the pools of the active Token A / Token B pair and reads slot0() and liquidity() of each pool (eth_call only; nothing is sent or signed). Price = 1.0001^tick, token1 per token0 after decimals, like the Uniswap rows above. Liquidity is the raw liquidity() value. Spread vs Uni = (DEX price − best Uniswap V3 price of the same pair) / best Uni price × 100, the best Uni V3 pool being the priced, tradeable one with the most liquidity; "—" when no Uni V3 pool is priced. A fee tier without a pool is skipped; a failed read shows "error" and keeps the last good values. Ramses pools are keyed by tick spacing (ts) and report their own fee.

Not started.

Read-only (job 10). The two ALP pools (fee 0.0001%, no hook) are read every 30 s alongside the V3 reads, in one JSON-RPC batch of eth_calls to the selected chain. On Base: PositionManager.poolKeys(bytes25) for the PoolKey (verified by hashing it back to the poolId), StateView.getSlot0 and getLiquidity. On a PoolManager without a StateView (Robinhood Chain): the Pool.State words through extsload, with the PoolKey verified against the poolId. Price = (sqrtPriceX96 / 2^96)^2. The same rows appear as "ALP V4" in the Cross-DEX table above.

Pools marked STALE, EMPTY or UNKNOWN are left out of the spreads. A sell leg that quotes 0 marks its route RED.
